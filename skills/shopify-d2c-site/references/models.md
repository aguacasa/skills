# Models and thinking levels

| Work | Model | Thinking / effort |
|---|---|---|
| First build: section architecture, 3D scenes, motion system | Opus | high to max |
| Layout and scroll bugs (scroller, stacking contexts, clipping) | Opus | high |
| Design review rounds; copy and compliance edits | Opus or Sonnet | medium |
| Legal and policy drafts (then human review) | Sonnet | medium |
| One-line fixes, status checks, deploys | Haiku or Sonnet | low |

Pay for thinking when there's a system to design or a bug you can't see. Drop down once the change is obvious.
