# Optional preset: Restrained product storefront

A high-contrast product-led direction, selected by the user rather than imposed
on every store. Adapt to the supplied brand and accessibility requirements.

- Real packaging and product colors supply the visual identity.
- One standout hero moment per page; use 3D only when product geometry, assets,
  budget, and device performance make it worthwhile.
- A small number of scroll-led sections with gradual transitions. Provide complete
  reduced-motion alternatives and preserve legible text throughout.
- Avoid ornamental eyebrows, outlined background type, floating debris, cursor
  followers, decorative drips, and redundant hint text. Keep meaningful labels
  and interaction instructions when usability requires them.
- Use solid contrast, readable controls, and restrained typography. Text should
  remain clear of objects and effects at all viewport sizes.

This is a visual preset, not a supplement business model, theme selection,
regulatory policy, or instruction to copy the demo's products.
