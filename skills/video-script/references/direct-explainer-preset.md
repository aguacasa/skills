# Optional preset: Direct explainer

Choose this only when the user selects it or their brief clearly requests it.
It contains style choices, not a personal identity, niche, or biography.

- Plain, direct, conversational language with a restrained first take.
- Explain unfamiliar terms for casual viewers while giving experienced viewers
  something practical: a decision, comparison, checklist, or small experiment.
- Vary short, punchy sentences with longer, flowing explanations. Avoid forced
  humor, elaborate metaphors, exaggerated agreement, and polished ad language.
- Open with a clear promise. Build a little recognition and explain why it matters
  before the main method, when that structure suits the topic. Do not delay for its own sake.
- Bold or provocative hooks are optional. Qualify them quickly and make the body
  deliver; do not invent evidence or controversy.
- Keep the creator's natural wording. Finish with a complete, copyable script.
- Use a relevant audience question and an optional short follow line when requested.

Ask what to keep or change. The user's own examples and current instructions take
precedence. Pair with the clean-explainer video preset only if selected separately.
